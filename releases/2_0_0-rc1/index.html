vega-webgpu-renderer

Version 2.0.0-rc1

Built for Vega 6. Every mark type is drawn, most edges are antialiased analytically rather than by MSAA, and text goes through a glyph atlas.

vega-webgpu-renderer.js vega-webgpu-renderer.min.js ESM build Try it

Load it

This build targets Vega 6. Loaded next to a different major version of Vega it throws when it registers itself. 

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/vega@6/build/vega.min.js"></script>
<script src="https://kanadaat.github.io/vega-webgpu/releases/2_0_0-rc1/vega-webgpu-renderer.min.js"></script>

Features

  • Trail marks, so all twelve of vega mark types render
  • Every value canvas takes in blend, the sixteen mix modes and the Porter Duff operators, on every mark
  • strokeDash on every mark that strokes
  • butt, round and square strokeCap, and miter, round and bevel strokeJoin with strokeMiterLimit
  • Gradient fills on arc, area, group, path, rect, shape, symbol, text and trail
  • Gradient strokes on every mark that strokes
  • strokeForeground, so a group border can draw over its children
  • sampleCount, to pick 4x MSAA or none
  • redrawOnZoom, to follow browser zoom or hold the ratio the canvas was first sized at
  • canvasTextDrift, to place a label where the canvas renderer places it rather than where its own coordinates say
  • The canvas fits itself to the GPU maximum texture size instead of failing, and asks the adapter for its own limit, 16384 on most desktop GPUs, rather than the 8192 default

Performance

  • Text draws from a single glyph atlas: a rotating radial tree went from 328 ms a frame to 8.5
  • Cubic curves are evaluated on the GPU rather than flattened on the cpu
  • Item geometry, pipelines and uniform buffers are cached between frames
  • A frame no longer drains the GPU before the next one starts
  • A frame frees the buffers its draws used
  • About 14x faster than the canvas renderer at three hundred thousand points
  • A frame that changes little does little: shape outlines, label metrics, area, trail and clip fills, pipelines and uniforms are kept between frames

Fixes

  • Rules, segments, rects, circles and the symbol shapes with a closed form take analytic edge coverage, so an abutting edge no longer leaves a seam
  • An svg path is read through vega own path renderer, so a path mark takes the commands canvas takes, including an arc past 240 degrees
  • A stroke joins and caps the way canvas does: both segments at a vertex keep their own side of one bisector, two facing round caps across a dash gap meet at the midpoint, and a dash cut near a corner no longer leaves the stroke cut off along the inside of it
  • A trail stroke is composited once rather than once per segment, so a translucent or blended one no longer darkens at every joint
  • A blend is exact at any source alpha and over any backdrop, including none
  • A reduced image takes a mip chain with anisotropy rather than four texels
  • Glyphs land on the sub-pixel phase canvas puts them on
  • A group border takes vega half pixel stroke offset
  • A path stops flattening its curves into facets
  • A path that will not tessellate falls back instead of vanishing
  • An arc that sets no innerRadius keeps its cornerRadius, instead of closing the shape on a NaN
  • strokeCap square no longer drops strokeDash on arc, area, path and trail, and a shape keeps the cap when its stroke is solid
  • A gradient filled symbol takes its blend on the stroke as well as the fill
  • A NaN coordinate lands at the origin, the way vega reads it, and a fully transparent colour no longer reaches the frame as NaN
  • A line past six and a half thousand points draws instead of throwing
  • Swapping renderers, or taking over an element, releases the GPU device that was held
  • Each label of a text mark takes its own blend, where every label took the first one
  • A group mark with clip true is clipped, and a mark clipped inside a rounded group follows the corners
  • Step, open, closed and gapped lines take round caps and joins, where their corners were mitered and their ends cut square
  • renderBatch and simpleLine are gone: the line mark batches unconditionally
